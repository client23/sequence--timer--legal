Optional Supporter Unlock

PhaseKit is free to download and use for building and running timer routines. The optional one-time Support Developer purchase unlocks cosmetic app color customization and helps support ongoing maintenance.

This is not a subscription. Core timer features remain free.

Previous Paid App Customers

If you bought PhaseKit before it changed to a free download, you should not need to pay again for the supporter unlock. The app checks Apple-provided App Store transaction information and automatically unlocks supporter color customization for customers whose original app download was from the paid version.

If the unlock does not appear, open Settings in the app and use Restore.

Purchases, Billing, and Refunds

All purchases and refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store. PhaseKit does not receive or store your payment card details.

For purchase history, billing, or refund requests, use Apple's App Store purchase support:

https://support.apple.com/billing

Product Scope

PhaseKit is an interval and routine timer for custom sequences, grouped timers, saved routines, and focused timer playback.
